# App Ecosystem Sentiment & Developer Responsiveness Analysis

This repository contains a comprehensive Business Intelligence (BI) project focused on analyzing the relationship between user sentiment and developer engagement across a large-scale app marketplace. By correlating review reliability with developer response rates, the project identifies key drivers of app success and user trust.

## Project Overview
The primary objective of this analysis is to evaluate the "App Landscape" through the lens of user reviews and developer interaction. The study explores how responsiveness (developer answers) impacts average ratings and identifies top-tier developers who lead in both volume and sentiment.

## Key Features
*   **Sentiment Correlation Modeling:** A scatter plot analysis identifying a strong positive correlation between high user ratings and review reliability scores.
*   **Developer Responsiveness Tracking:** Comparative analysis of average ratings for apps where developers actively answer reviews versus those that remain unresponsive.
*   **Competitive Benchmarking:** A leaderboard of top developers (e.g., **Hextom, Privy, Bold**) ranked by their total accumulated rating volume and helpful review counts.
*   **Interaction Metrics:** Granular tracking of "Helpful Reviews" (Avg: **5.48**) and total app IDs (**7.341K**) to quantify the scale of the ecosystem.

## Tech Stack & Tools
*   **Power BI:** Utilized for end-to-end data modeling, DAX calculations, and interactive dashboarding (App Landscape, Reviews, and App Reviews+ pages).
*   **Data Engineering:** Implementation of filters and calculated fields for `average_of_rating`, `reviews_count`, and `developer_answered` status.

## Analysis Summary
The data reveals that **higher user ratings align with more consistent and dependable feedback**, reinforcing the value of reliable reviews in the marketplace. Furthermore, the analysis highlights that developers who maintain a high volume of interaction (answering reviews) tend to sustain higher average ratings, suggesting that **proactive engagement** is a critical factor in long-term app ecosystem health.

# Financial Performance & Operational Expense Analysis (June–July 2021)

This repository contains a comprehensive financial model for a service-based business, analyzing the relationship between service income and operational overhead. By segmenting data by month and category, this project provides a clear view of net profitability and cash flow during a high-growth period.

## Project Overview
The core objective of this analysis is to evaluate the business's financial health by contrasting high-volume service revenue against fixed and variable expenses. The project transforms raw transaction logs into actionable insights, identifying the primary drivers of both profit and cost.

## Key Features
*   **Income vs. Expense Modeling:** A comparative analysis of total revenue (**$81,739**) against operational expenses (**$27,404**) for June and July.
*   **Service Tier Analytics:** Identification of top revenue generators, specifically **Haircut by Senior Stylist ($21,109.50)** and **Haircut and Styling ($16,944.00)**.
*   **Overhead Segmentation:** Granular tracking of major cost centers, with **Salary ($21,048)** identified as the primary operational expense, followed by **Marketing ($2,499)** and **Rent ($1,167)**.
*   **Product Performance:** Evaluation of retail add-ons like **Shampoo with Conditioner ($931.50)** and **Towels ($799.00)** as secondary income streams.

## Tech Stack & Tools
*   **Excel Data Engineering:** Utilized for cleaning raw transaction data, extracting month-specific filters, and managing discount logic (e.g., **15% discount** on specific products).
*   **Data Visualization:** Implementation of bar charts and pie charts to visualize the distribution of expenses versus income across various service and product categories.

## Analysis Summary
The data demonstrates a robust service model where **Senior Stylist services** drive over 25% of total platform income. While the business maintains a healthy margin, the analysis highlights that **Salaries account for over 76% of total expenses**, suggesting that future profitability will depend heavily on optimizing staff productivity and increasing high-margin product sales like **Shampoo** and **Gel**.

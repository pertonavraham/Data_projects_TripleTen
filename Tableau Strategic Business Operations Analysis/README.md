# Strategic Business Operations Analysis

This repository contains a comprehensive data analysis and strategic roadmap for optimizing business profitability and reducing operational loss. By evaluating product performance across shipping modes and identifying high-return patterns, this project provides data-driven recommendations for portfolio management.

## Project Overview
The primary goal of this analysis is to identify "Profit Centers" and "Loss Makers" within the product catalog. The study offers specific directives on which categories to scale and which to decommission to maximize overall net margin.

## Key Features
*   **Profitability Tiering:** Categorization of high-performing sub-categories like **Copiers ($76,304)** and **Phones ($56,053)** versus loss-leading categories such as **Tables (-$23,067)**.
*   **Logistics Optimization:** Analysis of shipping modes showing that **Phones and Chairs** are most profitable in **Standard Class**, while **Tables** consistently lose money in that same mode.
*   **Return Rate Diagnostics:** Identification of products and specific customers (e.g., RM-19750 and HH-15010) with a **100% return rate**, allowing for targeted policy adjustments.
*   **Geographic Ad Budgeting:** A monthly regional heat map identifying peak performance combinations, such as **Indiana in October** and **Vermont in November**, for optimized marketing spend.

## Tech Stack & Tools
*   **Tableau / Data Visualization:** Utilized for multi-axial scatter plots comparing **Avg. Profit** vs. **Avg. Quantified Returns**.
*   **Business Intelligence:** Implementation of "Stop Selling" lists based on deep-dive financial analysis of specific SKUs like the **GBC DocuBind P400**, which accounted for over **$20,000 in losses**.

## Strategic Recommendations
The data indicates a clear shift is required: the organization should **stop doing business with Tables and Supplies** and refocus resources on high-margin, low-return categories like **Art, Envelopes, and Labels**. By prioritizing **Copiers, Phones, and Accessories**, the business can capitalize on its strongest profit drivers while eliminating systemic losses in the **First Class Machine** and **Standard Class Table** segments.
